About

I like moving into new areas and getting up to speed by building things. I joined ReDS Lab in the second year of my master's, new to LLM agents, and co-first-authored two papers within a year, including one at ICML 2026. Both started from a vague concern about how agents behave, which I turned into a testable question and then built the evaluation to measure. I want to keep doing this kind of investigation on frontier systems.
